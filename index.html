FAQ

Questions people ask first.

We're not a tech company. Is this for us?

Most of our clients aren't. If your team spends hours a week on tasks that follow the same steps every time, there's almost certainly something worth automating. The consultation will tell you what.

Do we need to change the software we use?

Rarely. We build on top of the CRM, inbox, accounting and calendar you already have. If a tool genuinely needs replacing we'll say so, but that's the exception.

Is this replacing our staff?

It takes the parts of their day they'd happily give up: data entry, chasing, copying between tools, answering the same question again. The people stay on the work that needs a person.

What happens when the AI gets something wrong?

Every system we build has a clear fallback: it flags a person, takes a message, or waits for approval. AI steps are scoped to decisions they can make reliably, and anything else is routed to a human.

Do we own what you build?

Yes. The accounts are in your name, the workflows live in your workspace, and the documentation is written for someone who isn't us. You can run it, change it, or hire someone else to.

What does it cost?

The consultation is free. Builds are fixed-price, scoped in writing, and sized to the project; a single workflow and a full department rollout are very different numbers. Running costs are mostly the software platforms' own fees, which we list in the scope.
